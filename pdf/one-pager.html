The forum

One day in New York to compare how Brazil and the US build, finance and regulate real estate.

80 invited leaders of Brazilian real estate (developers, CEOs, CIOs and fund managers) sit down with their American counterparts. Brazilian projects that would hold up anywhere often fail to reach global institutional capital, and the reason is rarely the return. Five panels and two presentations look at what Brazil can change in governance, reporting and capital structure so those investors can come in.
